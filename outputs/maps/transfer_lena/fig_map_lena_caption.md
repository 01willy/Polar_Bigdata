# fig_map_lena

Static 1 km maps of active layer thickness (ALT) for the Lena delta treated as a new region (source excludes Lena and a 100 km buffer), from ERA5-Land 2015–2020 climatology and the CCI ALT 1997–2021 mean. Labels are point or site-mean observations; map values are 1 km cell predictions. Grey: CCI PFR 1997–2021 mean below 10% or missing, surface water occurrence of 50% or more, sea, or missing input. Methods were fixed by pre-registered rules before mapping: a, R0 or the best grid-computable physics baseline if superior to P0 and not inferior in Lena, otherwise P0; b and c, R1 if superior to P1 (and to P1*) and not inferior in Lena, otherwise the recalibration reference. 90% interval: hierarchical conformal quantile of leave-one-region-out log errors (q = 0.71, 4 source regions); width is proportional to the prediction. a, P0 (source Stefan coefficient). b, R1 (shrunk anchor + residual CatBoost), one draw of 10 labels (split 1); recalibrated coefficient 1.48–1.74 over 5 draws (area-mean ALT 43–50 cm), 1.38–1.97 over 5 splits (39–56 cm). c, R1 (shrunk anchor + residual CatBoost), all Lena labels. d, 90% interval width of a. e, covariates outside the source 0.5–99.5 percentile range, missing included (most often e5_maat, e5_fdd, e5_tcold); not an error-risk indicator. f, c minus a. LGT/LGF learner comparisons not included. P0@tddm is undefined on a static grid; P0 was used. 38,086 of 53,011 cells shown. Of 38,173 land cells, 87 (0.2%) are grey (CCI ALT missing 77, PFR missing 10). LAEA projection centred at 72.5°N, 126.7°E.
