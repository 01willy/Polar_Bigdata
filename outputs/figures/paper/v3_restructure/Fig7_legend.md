**Within Alaska, residual ML gains over recalibrated Stefan were below 1 cm and came from climate-grid-scale bias correction.** **a**, Within-region tests (half of the 0.5° blocks scored, 25 splits): error change of Anchor + residual ML (cross-validated residual weight) and Direct ML relative to Stefan recalibrated on the same labels. Error floor: covariate-conditional floor (Alaska 11.31, Lena Delta 14.83, Canada 17.90 cm) minus recalibrated Stefan RMSE; the Canadian line (−10.6 to −10.8 cm) lies below the axis. In Alaska with 1000 labels the residual model had 0.54 cm lower error (14.41 to 13.87 cm; 95% CI 0.32 to 0.69 cm), 19% of the squared error above the floor; reductions with 500 and all labels (0.39 and 0.52 cm) did not hold under the common-resampling interval. No difference was established in the Lena Delta or Canada. **b**, Alaska with all labels: error change by scoring block (25 splits summed) and the 343 label locations; blocks with under ten scored cells masked. **c**, Error change split between and within ERA5-Land climate cells. In Alaska about 99% of the squared-error reduction came from the between-cell component (−1.03 cm; within-cell −0.01 cm); in the three-region mean, within-cell predictions increased error by 0.09 cm. **d**, Recommended method per label interval: error change relative to the source-coefficient Stefan model, Lena Delta and Canada transfer pool (two-region mean of point estimates; 320 and 1000 labels not tested); placement and diagnosis steps below; selection from 40 labels rests on non-inferiority to a fixed recipe (Fig. 4c). **e**, Workflow (d) versus random placement with the fixed recipe (−0.86, −0.93 cm; 40, 160 labels) and versus recalibrated Stefan (+0.08, −1.35, −1.51 cm; 10, 40, 160 labels); symbols, regions over 0.5 cm worse than recalibrated Stefan; designed after results were inspected; re-test in reused regions. Intervals: 95% block-bootstrap confidence intervals (10,000 resamples of 0.5° scoring blocks), weighted by cell and by block (key); ±0.5 cm band, equivalence margin. **a** to **d** were designed after the transfer results had been inspected. Polar stereographic projection centred on Alaska, scale bar true at 70° N; coastlines, Natural Earth 50 m; Cartopy 0.23.0.
