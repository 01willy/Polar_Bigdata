# Fig. 1

**Study regions, Stefan coefficients and evaluation design.** a, Labelled cells of the licence-verified label tables in 0.5° blocks; circle area is proportional to the number of 1 km label locations in a block, and New regions marks the regions added after the label tables were fixed (Central Russia, expanded edition; Tibetan Plateau, inset; Table 1). Shading shows continuous (permafrost fraction at least 90%) and discontinuous (50–90%) permafrost (ESA CCI Permafrost fraction v4.0, 1997–2021 mean). The rectangle marks the area of c and d. b, Stefan coefficient E = ALT/√TDD of labelled cells by region (logarithmic axis), where ALT is the active-layer thickness and TDD the ERA5-Land air thawing index (°C d, 2015–2020 mean); up to 400 cells per region with median and interquartile range; Source coefficient is the coefficient fitted on the source pool when that region is held out. c, Region holdout: the labelled cells of the Lena Delta and all cells within 100 km of them (Buffer) are removed from the training data. d, Split of the same region into label blocks and scoring blocks (one of five splits) with 40 label cells drawn from the label blocks; errors are scored only in the scoring blocks. The within-region tests use the same block split without source data and were designed after the transfer results had been inspected. e, RMSE of direct ML and of the recalibrated Stefan model under random-cell, site, 0.5° block, kNNDM and region-holdout validation against the median distance from scoring cells to the nearest training cell; [XH: Fig 1e | 결과 열람 뒤 설계, 서술(판정어 없음) | 2.8 사전 고정 서술 규칙]. Cells without a verified licence (Canada 38, North Atlantic 19, W Russia 1) are not drawn. Polar stereographic projection (true scale at 70° N, central meridian 127° E), scale bar true at 70° N; c and d share this projection; Tibetan Plateau inset in a Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection with its own scale bar. Coastlines, Natural Earth 50 m (10 m in c and d); maps drawn with Cartopy 0.23.
