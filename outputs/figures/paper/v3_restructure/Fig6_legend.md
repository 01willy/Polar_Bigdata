**Without target labels, 2 of 30 targets lost over 2 cm with physics pseudo-labels and 18 with direct ML.** **a**, **b**, Error change relative to the source-coefficient Stefan model for direct ML (**a**) and direct ML with physics pseudo-labels (**b**), at each target's labelled-cell centre (17 targets; source excludes the parent region). Values below −40 cm take the end colour (Tibetan Plateau in **a**, −59.98 cm). Alaska targets enlarged; Lena Delta targets offset with leader lines. Title counts are post hoc over 30 targets (adding within-region sub-regions and three expanded-label editions; point-estimate threshold). **c**, Direct ML by learner and two physics baselines, four-region mean. The random forest, CatBoost with physics-model inputs, and four neural networks configured by leave-one-source-region-out cross-validation had higher error (2.44–5.21 cm). The other five learners, including the main CatBoost learner (2.25 cm; 95% CI 1.10 to 3.42 cm; Holm-adjusted P = 0.023), had higher error under the primary intervals, dependent on the split-independence assumption. Year-matched Stefan had 1.00 cm lower error, also dependent on that assumption. Anchor ensemble, mean of scale-calibrated Stefan, Kudryavtsev and ESA CCI anchors. Existing ALT maps (added post hoc; blocks within 5 km of product training sites removed): ESA CCI v5 had higher error (+23.79 cm at axis end; 95% CI 15.34 to 30.16); the Wei v2 difference (Lena Delta and Canada; 47% of cells gap-filled) was not resolved. **d**, Coverage of 90% prediction intervals against mean width without labels for five interval scalings: four-region mean (95% bootstrap intervals, 1000 resamples) and regional values; stacked labels, two near-coincident means (upper, higher coverage); line, nominal 0.90. **e**, Coverage of residual-model intervals with 40 and 160 labels against width relative to zero labels; band, 0.85 to 0.95. Intervals in **c** and **e**: 95% block-bootstrap confidence intervals (10,000 resamples of 0.5° scoring blocks), in **c** weighted by cell and block (key); ±0.5 cm band, equivalence margin. Polar stereographic projections (central meridian 127° E; Alaska enlargement, 157° W), scale bars true at 70° N; Tibetan Plateau inset, Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection. Permafrost zones, ESA CCI Permafrost fraction v4.0 (1997–2021 mean); coastlines, Natural Earth 50 m; Cartopy 0.23.0.
