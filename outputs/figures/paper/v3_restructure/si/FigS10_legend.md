**Method selection: target-wise choices of the cross-validation rule and source cross-validation tuning of neural networks.** **a**, Method chosen most often by five-fold block cross-validation within ten target labels (25 selections per target and scoring mode, 5 splits × 5 draws; both scoring modes pooled for sub-regions). The rule was designed after the label-grid results had been viewed. **b**, RMSE of the rule minus that of the fixed residual recipe (anchor plus residual ML, residual weight 0.25) with ten labels (point estimates); values beyond ±3 cm take the end colours (Tibetan Plateau, −29.53 cm). **c**, Neural networks configured by leave-one-source-region-out cross-validation minus their default configuration, four-region stratified mean, for residual ML (weights 0.25 and 1.0) and direct ML, without labels, with ten labels and with all labels; each column has its own axis. **d**, Change in source cross-validation error against the change in target error without labels (32 points: four networks, two model kinds, four regions; Spearman ρ = −0.32, descriptive). At 12 points tuning kept the default configuration (open circle). Intervals, 95% block-bootstrap confidence intervals weighted by cell (thick) and block (thin); band, ±0.5 cm. Map conventions as in Supplementary Fig. S8.
