**Validation ladder in Alaska: split maps, distance distributions and RMSE under four scoring schemes.** **a** to **d**, Assignment of the 13,606 labelled 1 km cells to six folds for one split (seed 0): random cells, 0.05° sites, 0.5° blocks and k-fold nearest-neighbour distance matching (kNNDM; 13 k-means clusters merged into six folds). **e** to **h**, Distance from each test cell to the nearest training cell (blue) and from 20,000 sampled permafrost cells of the 0.02° prediction grid (mean annual air temperature below 0 °C) to the nearest label (grey), as share of cells per logarithmic distance bin (seed 0). Median test-to-training distances (mean of three seeds) were 0.004, 1.11, 25.8 and 53.5 km; the median map-to-label distance was 81.6 km. **i**, RMSE (mean of three seeds) of direct ML (CatBoost), the Stefan model with the coefficient fitted to the training folds, and the Stefan model plus a ridge residual (weight 0.75). From random cells to kNNDM, the RMSE of direct ML rose from 11.6 to 17.9 cm, that of the Stefan model from 14.2 to 14.5 cm and that of the residual model from 12.77 to 13.70 cm. Alaska Albers equal-area projection; coastlines, Natural Earth 50 m.
