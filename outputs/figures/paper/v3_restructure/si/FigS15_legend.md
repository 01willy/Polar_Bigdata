**Deployment scenarios: scenario matrix, sequential stopping rule and abstract contrast bundle.** **a**, Four-way verdict by stage (labels in the new region) and contrast for five regions and the four-region stratified mean, from 95% block-bootstrap intervals weighted by cell and by block with an equivalence margin of ±0.5 cm; frames, non-inferior with margin 0.5 cm; hatching, more labels than the region provides; dagger, pool of Lena Delta and Canada. Source, source-coefficient Stefan model; recalibrated, recalibrated Stefan model. **b**, Sequential stopping rule for recalibration (3, 10, then 40 labels; stop when the jackknife standard error of the log coefficient is at most τ): RMSE change relative to always using 40 labels against the mean share of labels used, three-region mean (Lena Delta, Canada, Alaska) with cell-weighted 95% intervals, and regional values. With τ = 0.05 the rule used 0.91 of the labels and changed RMSE by −0.05 cm (95% CI −0.08 to −0.002). **c**, Abstract contrast bundle in the order of Supplementary Table S3: four-region stratified mean with 95% block-bootstrap intervals weighted by cell (thick) and block (thin), regional values (symbols), and the wording rule after Holm correction over ten contrasts. Ensemble, mean of scale-calibrated Stefan, Kudryavtsev and ESA CCI anchors. The last row compares interval scores in the Lena Delta, Canada and Alaska (+9.51 cm; 95% CI −0.75 to 22.11). Horizontal axis linear within ±2 cm and logarithmic beyond.
