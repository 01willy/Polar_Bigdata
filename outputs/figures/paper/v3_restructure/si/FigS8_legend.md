**Transfer without target labels: target-wise error change of the residual models and learner-wise detail.** **a**, **b**, Error change relative to the source-coefficient Stefan model without target labels for the source anchor plus residual ML with residual weight 0.25 (low-weight residual, **a**) and 1.0 (**b**), at each target's labelled-cell centre (17 targets; source excludes the parent region; post hoc re-analysis of the label-grid curves, CatBoost). Values below −40 cm take the end colour (Tibetan Plateau in **b**, −47.34 cm). Alaska targets enlarged; Lena Delta targets offset with leader lines. **c**, Error change relative to the source-coefficient Stefan model without target labels for direct ML (ten learners, TabICL v2 with two context settings, and CatBoost with physics inputs) and for the low-weight residual (three learners): four-region stratified mean and region rows; each column has its own axis. CatBoost (main) uses the abstract-bundle contrast for the four regions and the label-grid curve for Alaska. Intervals, 95% block-bootstrap confidence intervals weighted by cell (thick) and by 0.5° block (thin); band, ±0.5 cm. Polar stereographic projections (central meridian 127° E; Alaska enlargement, 157° W), scale bars true at 70° N; Tibetan Plateau inset, Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection. Permafrost zones, ESA CCI Permafrost fraction v4.0 (1997–2021 mean); coastlines, Natural Earth 50 m.
