**Gains within label-rich regions: error change by scoring block, high-resolution inputs, climate extrapolation and stacking.** **a**, **b**, Error change of residual ML (cross-validated residual weight, mean of two seeds) relative to the Stefan model recalibrated on the same labels, by 0.5° scoring block, within-region test with all labels (half of the blocks scored per split; Lena Delta 20 blocks over 24 splits, Canada 36 blocks over 25 splits; split means +0.47 and +0.45 cm). In **b** each block is drawn as an enlarged square at its centre; one Canadian block (+23.77 cm) takes the end colour. **c**, Residual ML with ten additional within-grid inputs minus residual ML with the baseline inputs, and minus recalibrated Stefan, three-region stratified mean (500 and 1000 labels, Alaska and Lena Delta only). **d**, Spatial proxy for climate extrapolation in Canada (warm blocks scored): extrapolation penalty of direct ML relative to recalibrated Stefan, penalty of residual ML relative to direct ML, and residual ML minus recalibrated Stefan in the warm blocks; filled, main edition (extrapolation width 0.97 in √TDD units); open, basic edition (width −0.002). **e**, Label-weighted stacking of physics models and products minus recalibrated Stefan, and stacked residual minus residual, in the transfer pool and within regions. Intervals, 95% block-bootstrap confidence intervals weighted by cell (thick) and block (thin); band, ±0.5 cm. All analyses were designed after earlier results had been viewed. Polar stereographic projections; coastlines, Natural Earth 50 m.
