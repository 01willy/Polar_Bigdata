**Placement of new labels: target-wise strategy contrasts, proximity of labels and learned placement policies.** **a**, **b**, Error change of residual ML (anchor plus residual, weight 0.25) when ten transfer labels are placed by block stratification (**a**) or by covariate maximin-distance spreading (**b**) instead of at random cells (13 targets with a placement test; grey, not tested); values above 4 cm take the end colour (Tibetan Plateau in **b**, +12.71 cm). **c**, Proximity contrasts, four-region stratified mean (symbols, regions): direct ML scored on a random split minus a block split; error relative to recalibrated Stefan with labels near the scored cells minus labels in held-out blocks, for direct ML and residual ML; and residual ML with ten labels inside the scored blocks minus outside them. **d**, Contrast in the nearest third of scored cells minus the farthest third, for residual ML minus recalibrated Stefan and recalibrated minus source Stefan (cell-weighted 95% intervals). **e**, Learned placement policy and three variants minus block stratification and minus covariate spreading (five-region mean with 10 labels; Lena Delta and Canada with 40 labels), the learned policy minus random cells, and the design-weighted coefficient minus the learned policy in the Lena Delta (two-stage 95% intervals). Placement analyses in **a**, **b** and **e** were designed after earlier results had been viewed. Intervals in **c** and **e**, 95% confidence intervals weighted by cell (thick) and block (thin); band, ±0.5 cm. Map conventions as in Supplementary Fig. S8.
