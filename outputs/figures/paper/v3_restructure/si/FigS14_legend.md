**Prediction intervals for the Lena Delta without target labels.** **a**, Active-layer thickness predicted by the source-coefficient Stefan model (source regions exclude the Lena Delta and a 100 km buffer; ERA5-Land 2015–2020 climatology). **b**, Width of the 90% prediction interval of **a**: hierarchical conformal quantile of leave-one-region-out log errors (q = 0.71; calibration regions Alaska, Canada, E Russia and W Russia), so the width is proportional to the prediction; with four calibration regions the interval carries no finite-sample guarantee. **c**, Number of covariates outside the 0.5–99.5 percentile range of the source rows, missing values included; all mapped cells have three or more (most often mean annual air temperature, freezing degree-days and coldest-month temperature). This count is not an error-risk indicator. 38,086 of 53,011 grid cells are shown; of 38,173 land cells, 87 (0.2%) lack inputs. 1 km display grid; north polar stereographic projection centred at 126.7° E; coastlines, Natural Earth 10 m.
