# Alaska observed versus predicted ALT (block cross-validation)

**Out-of-block predictions of active-layer thickness (ALT) at the 13,606 Alaska label cells.** Each cell is predicted by a model fitted without its 0.5° block (five-fold block cross-validation of the Alaska map model, 74 blocks; in each fold the Stefan coefficient and the residual weight λ are re-estimated within the training folds). a, observed against the recalibrated Stefan model; b, observed against the recalibrated anchor plus residual ML (the map model). Points are coloured by the number of cells in their 2 × 2 cm bin (log scale); grey line, 1:1. c, change in the mean absolute residual per block, residual ML minus recalibrated Stefan (negative, residual ML residuals smaller), drawn as 0.5° blocks; 47 of 74 blocks are negative; 38 blocks hold fewer than five cells. Colour range ±8 cm (99th percentile of |change|). Cell-weighted RMSE: recalibrated Stefan 14.30 cm, residual ML 13.80 cm (registered map record 14.30 and 13.80 cm); mean absolute residual 10.52 and 10.06 cm; bias −1.34 and −1.39 cm. Observed values above about 100 cm are few and are under-predicted by both models. Projection NAD83 / Alaska Albers; land grey, Natural Earth 10 m.
