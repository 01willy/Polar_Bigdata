# Lena Delta methods by label count

**1 km ALT of the Lena Delta from four methods fitted on the source pool (14,429 cells of other regions, Lena and a 100 km buffer removed) plus n Lena labels.** Rows, n = 10, 40, 160 labels: the first n cells of the seed-0 block-dispersed order used in the label-count map sequence (black points). Columns: recalibrated Stefan (E_n, κ = 10); anchor plus residual ML (λ 0.25); direct ML; physics pseudo-labels (ten pseudo rows per source row from the label half of split 1, value E_n·√TDD) plus the n labels. CatBoost, two seeds averaged. Text under each map: RMSE at all Lena label cells not drawn (held-out cells include cells in blocks that also hold drawn labels); vermillion frame, lowest of the four in the row. Right: choice of the cross-validation rule (seven candidates, five-fold block cross-validation within the drawn labels) and its held-out RMSE. One draw and one split only.
