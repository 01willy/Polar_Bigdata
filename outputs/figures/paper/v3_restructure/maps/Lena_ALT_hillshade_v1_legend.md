# Lena Delta ALT on hillshade

**Residual-ML active-layer thickness (ALT) of the Lena Delta at 1 km, draped on terrain relief.** Colour, ALT of the final residual-ML map (the registered Lena final map (h49 R1, λ = 0.25)), 35–50 cm on the same scale as the v3 map figure. Relief, hillshade of the Copernicus DEM GLO-30 averaged to 250 m in the map projection (azimuth 315°, altitude 45°, vertical exaggeration 2, 22 tiles), blended by multiplication with weight 0.35 (flat ground keeps the ALT colour). Land outside the mapped domain is grey with the same relief; white, sea or water; light grey, no data. The relief is display context only: the ALT value of each 250 m pixel is that of its 1 km cell, and the climate inputs behind the map are ERA5-Land 0.1° (about 10 km). Coastline, Natural Earth 10 m. Projection and extent as in the v3 map figure.
