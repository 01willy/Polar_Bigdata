# Lena Delta transfer maps without target labels

**1 km active-layer thickness (ALT) of the Lena Delta predicted without Lena labels by three methods, and their errors at the 3037 Lena label cells.** All fits use only the 14,429 source label cells of the other regions (Lena and a 100 km buffer removed), so every Lena label cell is a test point. a, source-coefficient Stefan E0·√TDD, E0 = 1.619. b, direct ML: CatBoost (200 iterations, depth 3, two seeds averaged) on the 25 covariates of the source rows. c, physics pseudo-label augmentation: the same learner on the source rows plus 144,290 pseudo rows (ten per source row) drawn with replacement from the 1505 cells of the label half of registered split 1, valued E0·√TDD. d, observed against predicted ALT (grey line, 1:1). e–g, prediction minus observed per label cell. h, residual distribution (5 cm bins). Colour scales: ALT 40–65 cm (1st–99th percentile of a–c), residual ±75 cm (99th percentile). RMSE at all label cells: 21.70, 25.51 and 21.91 cm (a, b, c); on the registered scoring halves, mean of five splits: 21.69, 25.68, 21.88 cm (registered record 21.69, 25.71, 21.88 cm). White, water; grey, no data.
